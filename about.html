Frequently Asked Questions

How does the matching algorithm work?

Our algorithm compares lost and found items based on category, description similarity, color, location proximity, and time factors. It assigns a match score and notifies users when high-confidence matches are found.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes! We only share contact information after successful verification. Your email and phone number are never publicly displayed and are only shared with matched users.

What if I lost my verification code?

You can access your verification codes through your dashboard. If you're still having trouble, contact our support team for assistance.

How long does matching take?

Matching runs automatically every 30 seconds. However, it depends on whether similar found items have been reported. Some matches happen instantly, while others may take longer.

What items can I report?

You can report any lost or found item! Common categories include electronics, clothing, accessories, documents, keys, books, bags, jewelry, and more.

Is this service really free?

Absolutely! Lost & Found is completely free to use. We believe in helping communities and don't charge for reuniting people with their belongings.
